04

Elements as mechanism

Each element is a color, but also a way of behaving. The same four traits that define them spiritually define how they act in the interface.

火

Fire · Bold

Red at full strength on primary actions. Decisive, committed, impossible to ignore — the strike.

水

Water · Fluid

Glassmorphism itself — panels that morph and pool light on hover, flowing rather than fixed.

土

Earth · Grounded

Wide, low shadows that root elements into the page rather than floating them above it.

風

Air · Formless

The motion language — fade and rise, ease-out, never bounce. Everything arrives like breath.

05

The void stage

Pure black is a stage, not a background — opt in with .void where a mark or figure has to carry. Ink stays the reading surface. Note the hairlines below are stronger here than elsewhere: --line re-binds inside the scope, because a border at ink strength starts dissolving on black.

Element marks — geometric register

Original marks, not the Avatar ones. One 24-grid, 1.5 stroke, butt caps, no circle enclosure. Each is open in the way its element is open — fire's apex is cut and still travelling, air is only corners.
